## Marketing Promise

The 5 Spheres of Empathy is for people who believe that in an age of AI, the human skills are the
ones that matter most: knowing what you truly value, reading people clearly, and leading with care
when everything is harder, messier, and faster than ever before.

We focus on people who want to train those skills in human intelligence, not just read about them.

We promise you a place to do the work: the method and the Field Guide, monthly skill drills,
community events, a growing library of free tools, and the chance to work with one of our coaches,
and soon, to train as one yourself.

## Who it is for

Leaders, founders and professionals who are commercially competent and know something is not
working. The gap is between the impact they want and the impact they have. Their calendar is
largely decided by other people, so they make real decisions from a state nobody has asked them
about. They would rather be told something accurate than something encouraging.

**Who it is not for.** Anyone looking for a productivity system, anyone who wants accountability
chasing, and anyone who needs it to be comfortable every time.

---

## Tone

**Accurate before encouraging.** The offer page says it plainly: the space is safe and it is not
always comfortable. We ask the question the person is steering around, gently, and we still ask it.

**Trained, not read.** The distinction in the promise is the whole brand. Most people think they
already understand empathy, and two hours with the method usually shows them they had it
backwards. We sell practice and scoring, not insight.

**Plain and short.** Outward copy runs two to four times too long on a first draft. Length reads as
need. Cut to the one thing about the reader's job.

**Human, in an age of AI.** We use AI in the work and we never position it as the thing doing the
work. The coach is a person. The drills are live. The scoring is yours.

## Language rules

**We say**
- "Human intelligence"
- "Train the skill" rather than "learn about it"
- "You bring a real decision. You leave deciding differently."
- "You score yourself before and after, so there is data rather than a feeling"
- "Most training is aimed at the people living with a decision. This is aimed at the decision."

**We never say**
- "Soft skills" as the category. The promise calls them the skills that matter most
- "Empathy training" where "skill drill" is accurate
- "Cohort" dates. The six-week format is withdrawn for individuals and returns in 2027 as
  something earned into through the drills
- "Guaranteed" anything about outcomes

**Mechanical rules, every surface.** No em dashes. No emojis. None of the three hedge words
banned in `VOICE.md`. No verbless punch and no staccato runs, both of which are blocking. Watch
mirror sentences, which are the commonest tell. The one command that checks all of it:
`python "%USERPROFILE%\.claude\brain\voice\voicepass.py" <file>`

## Visual identity

### Colour

Read from the brand PDF. These are the brand's own values and they are not the website's tokens.

| Token | Value | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | `#002c45` | Ink and dark ground. **Not the lead colour** |
| Red | `#e20000` | Accent |
| Off-white | `#f5e8d6` | Warm ground, paper |

**Ruled by G, 2 November 2026: the core colour is orange, meaning the sphere ramp below.** This
row previously read "Primary" against the blue, and it is the reason three rounds of social covers
were built on a blue ground before anyone said so. Anything leading with blue is reading this table
and stopping before the ramp.

Two consequences, measured rather than preferred:

- On `#ff6500`, off-white `#f5e8d6` is **2.45:1** and fails. Blue `#002c45` is **4.92:1** and
  passes. An orange ground takes blue type.
- On `#002c45`, the brand red `#e20000` is **3.1:1** and fails as text. Where red type is wanted on
  the blue, it has to be lightened to about `#ff6b6b`, which is 5.2:1.

**The sphere ramp**, warm, running outward from the self:

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| 1 | `#ff0000` |
| 2 | `#ff4900` |
| 3 | `#ff6500` |
| 4 | `#ff8400` |
| 5 | `#ffae3c` |
| 50% tint | `#fccb89` |

**Open:** the PDF labels Spheres 2 to 5 against five values, so the mapping of the first value to
Sphere 1 is inferred from the order. Confirm with the designer before the ramp is used to colour a
diagram by sphere.

## Where this brand sits

Three ventures, and the split is three-way.

- **5 Spheres of Empathy** is the people part. The method, the drills, the workshops, the books.
- **Optomize** is the technology company. It builds the platform.
- **HowLegit** is the not-for-profit public register of company claims.

5 Spheres sells to people, and to companies who want it for a team. It does not grade anyone and
it does not sell software. Where the coaching platform appears, the brand the buyer sees is 5
Spheres and the company behind the tech is Optomize.
