Coaching · five places

Human Intelligence, run one to one

Human Intelligence is guided practice on empathy as a practical skill, starting with yourself. This is the same programme, worked one to one, with the track built around the decisions you are actually carrying.

Who it is for

Leaders, founders and professionals who know something is not working

You are commercially competent, possibly successful, and there is a gap between the impact you want to have and the impact you are having. You care about the people your decisions land on, and the frameworks you have been given feel either too soft to use or too technical to be human.

Your calendar is largely decided by other people, so you are making real decisions from a state nobody has asked you about. You have a change in mind, in a role or a venture or a way of working, and it keeps not happening. You would rather be told something accurate than something encouraging.

What the work is

The inner two spheres, traced through a real decision of yours

Most people think they already understand empathy. Two hours with the method usually shows them they had it backwards. Understanding it and living it are different things, and closing that gap is what the programme is for.

The work runs through the first two spheres. The Vulnerable Self is what actually drives you, including the parts you are too busy to unpack. The Conscious Self is your capacity to act on it, which covers what state you are in when you decide and what you do differently on Monday morning.

You bring a real decision. You leave deciding differently.

Why one to one

The group version asks for six consecutive weeks

The cohort runs weekly for six weeks with eight to twelve people. That shape works when your diary is your own. For most of the people who ask about it, six consecutive weeks is the thing that never happens.

Run one to one, the programme keeps its content and loses its scheduling. Sessions move when your week moves. The track is built around your situation rather than around what a group of twelve has in common.

Straight about this

What it will not do

It will not give you more hours. If the schedule is the problem, the work is deciding what to stop, which is a harder conversation than a productivity system.

It will not be comfortable every time. The space is safe and it is not always comfortable. I will ask the question you are steering around, gently, and I will still ask it.

It will not work on accountability alone. I am not going to chase you. If a month goes by because your life went sideways, that is the format working. Nothing changes in a month where nothing changes.
