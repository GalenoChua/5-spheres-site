What I speak on

Empathy is intelligence about people

Almost every business problem is a people problem. The 5 Spheres of Empathy, from the self you bring to work out to the people who will inherit what you build.

Agree to Disagree

A live roundtable. Four people from different worlds take difficult statements, pick a side and defend it in the room. The audience votes too.

Sustainability, from first principles

Sustainability is empathy made concrete: vision, culture and values, then measurement and governance, run through a real organisation.

Each works as a keynote, a hands-on workshop or a panel, in person or online.
