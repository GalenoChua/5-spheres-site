When you go again. One person speaks for six minutes. The other only senses — no advice, no fixing, no reassurance. Scan three layers while they talk: physical (heart rate, tension, energy), mental (clear or fogged, racing or stuck), emotional (what the weather is in there, named plainly). Then reflect something back. Paraphrase it, or offer a hunch as a question — “I’m sensing… is that right?” Then swap.
