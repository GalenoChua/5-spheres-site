Zone 1, the scan. One person speaks for six or seven minutes. The other only senses — no advice, no fixing, no reassurance. Scan three layers as you go: physical (heart rate, tension, energy), mental (clear or fogged, racing or stuck), emotional (what is the weather in there, named plainly). Then reflect something back: paraphrase, or offer a hunch as a question — “I’m sensing… is that right?” Then swap.
