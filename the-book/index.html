The 5 Spheres of Empathy

How to Build Something That Outlives You

Almost every business problem is a people problem. Empathy is intelligence about people, starting with you. Not kindness, not sentiment, but a practical skill that helps you make better decisions, the kind that build something worth inheriting.

The 5 Spheres are five territories of attention, from the self you bring to work to the people who will inherit what you build: the Vulnerable Self, the Conscious Self, the Direct Stakeholder, Affected Indirect Stakeholders, and Those Who Come After.

About the author

Galeno Chua, Mr. G to most rooms, is a Kiwi-Singaporean social entrepreneur based in Warsaw. He has spent nearly two decades working with organisations from the United Nations and Oxfam to more than two hundred startups across ASEAN, Europe and New Zealand. His frameworks have been applied by Google.org funded startups, the New Zealand Government and ASEAN government programmes.
