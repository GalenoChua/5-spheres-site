Tools for Parents

Small tools, built for my own kids.

Teaching a seven-year-old to read a clock face takes more patience than either of you expects, and the apps that promise to help mostly want an account and an advert break. So I built one. It worked, so it is here, free, for anyone else sitting at a kitchen table with the same problem. More will follow as my children hand me the next thing they cannot do yet.

Available now

The tools

Telling the Time

Four levels, from o'clock and half past through to matching an analogue face against a digital one. The hands drag, so you can set your own questions by hand. English, Polish, Mandarin, Bahasa Indonesia and French, with pinyin you can switch off, and a history card for every hour on the clock.

Why these are here

Empathy is built or blocked at home, long before anyone calls it a skill

I work with leaders on reading a room, sitting with disagreement and staying present under pressure. Almost none of it starts at work. It starts with whether a child was listened to while they were getting something wrong, and whether the adult beside them stayed.

That is hard to do with nothing in your hands. Sitting down to teach a seven-year-old the clock gives you a reason to be there, a thing to look at together, and twenty minutes where you are both working on the same problem. The learning is real, and it is also the cover story. What the child remembers is that you sat down.

So these tools are small on purpose. One thing a child cannot do yet, in a form you can open and close in one sitting, with no account and nothing to buy. The tool is the structure. The time is the point.

If you use one, tell me how it went. Three questions, no names, and the answers decide what I build next.

How these work

Nothing is collected

There are no accounts, no adverts, no third party trackers and no analytics on these pages. Nothing you or your child does here is sent anywhere or seen by me. Each tool remembers two things, the level and the language you last used, and it keeps them in your own browser on your own device. Clear your browser data and they are gone. That is the whole of it.

If something is broken or wrong, or if there is a tool your child needs that is not here, tell me and I will look at it. If you have used one with your child, the three question form is the most useful thing you can send me.
