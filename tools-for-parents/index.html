Tools for Parents

Small tools, built for my own kids.

Teaching a seven-year-old to read a clock face takes more patience than either of you expects, and the apps that promise to help mostly want an account and an advert break. So I built one. It worked, so it is here, free, for anyone else sitting at a kitchen table with the same problem. More will follow as my children hand me the next thing they cannot do yet.

Available now

The tools

Telling the Time

Four levels, from o'clock and half past through to matching an analogue face against a digital one. The hands drag, so you can set your own questions by hand. English, Polish and Mandarin, with pinyin you can switch off, and a history card for every hour on the clock in five languages.

Open the app
How these work

Nothing is collected

There are no accounts, no adverts, no third party trackers and no analytics on these pages. Nothing you or your child does here is sent anywhere or seen by me. Each tool remembers two things, the level and the language you last used, and it keeps them in your own browser on your own device. Clear your browser data and they are gone. That is the whole of it.

If something is broken or wrong, or if there is a tool your child needs that is not here, tell me and I will look at it.
