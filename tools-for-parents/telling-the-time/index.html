How this works

Nothing is collected

No accounts, no adverts, no third party trackers and no analytics on this page. Nothing you or your child does here is sent anywhere. The app remembers four things in your own browser on your own device, the level, the app language, the trivia language and whether pinyin is showing, and it keeps them nowhere else. Clear your browser data and they are gone.

The one thing I would ask for is how it went. The feedback form is anonymous, it asks for no personal details and you send it only if you want to.
