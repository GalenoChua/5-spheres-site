Tools for Parents

Tell me how it went.

Three questions, no names, and you can skip any of them. I am not asking how good the tool is. I am asking what happened between you and your child while you used it, because that is the part the tool exists for and the part I cannot see from here.

Did it help?

Whether your child got further with it than they would have on their own.

Roughly how long were you sat together?

A guess is fine. Nobody times this.

What did your child learn?

In your words, however small. Please leave their name out.

1000 characters left

Thank you.

That is all I needed. Nothing else was sent and there is nothing to confirm.

What happens to this

Three answers, and nothing else

What gets stored is the three answers above and the time they arrived. No name, no email address, no IP address, no device details and no identifier of any kind, which also means there is no way to connect two answers to the same parent. I read them to decide which tool to build next and where the current ones lose a child.

There are still no accounts, no adverts, no third party trackers and no analytics on these pages. If you would rather write to me instead, hello@5spheresofempathy.com reaches me directly.
