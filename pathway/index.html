If it is one method, why two certifications

Same thinking. Different proof.

Sustainability is not a subject I teach alongside empathy. It is what empathy looks like once you extend it to people you will never meet and to a planet that cannot tell you what it needs. That is spheres four and five, and almost every sustainability problem turns out to be a people problem wearing a technical costume: the answers are mostly known and written down already, and what stops them is that someone did not want to say the uncomfortable thing.

So the craft does not change with the distance. How you listen, how you hold a room, how you give feedback that is actually heard, how you name the thing everyone has decided not to mention — that is the same work whether the person in front of you is a co-founder or a procurement lead. It is taught once and assessed once.

What differs is what you have to be able to show. A 5 Spheres coach has to work credibly across all five, including the inner two, which is its own kind of hard. A BSFP coach has to do it inside organisations that are paying for the answer, with enough substance in governance, reporting and environmental and social practice to be taken seriously in the room. Neither of those is a subset of the other, and neither is a reason to teach the craft twice.

If you hold one and later want the other, you add the evidence. You do not start again.

Before it is finished

The Business Sustainability Coach requirements are written and already public on the course page. The craft core and the 5 Spheres certification are not finished, and I would rather build those around people who intend to use them than hand you something already set.
