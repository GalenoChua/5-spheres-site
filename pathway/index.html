Why one core and not two

The overlap is craft, not subject matter.

When I laid the two tracks side by side I expected the shared part to be content. It was not. The sustainability material and the empathy material barely touch.

What is identical is everything a coach does regardless of what is being discussed: how you listen, how you hold a room, how you give feedback that is actually heard, how you name the thing everyone has decided not to mention. That is most of what separates a certified coach from a well-read one, and it does not change between the two tracks.

So it sits in the middle and is taught once. You are assessed on it once. If you come back later for the second track, you add the subject and the hours — not the craft again.

Before it is finished

Two of these are still being designed.

The BSFP requirements are written and I take applications for those now. The craft core and the 5 Spheres certification are not finished, and I would rather build them around people who intend to use them than hand you something already set. Tell me which one matters to you and I will come back to you as it takes shape.

This puts you on the list for the certification only. It does not sign you up to anything else.
