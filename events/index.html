Next up

Public Speaking

Thursday 22 October, 09:00 CEST · 90 minutes · Zoom · €50

Ninety minutes on holding a room. You speak, you get watched, and you get the twelve-point delivery checklist back against what the room actually saw rather than against how it felt from the inside.

The checklist runs from defensive energy before you open your mouth, through the hook, breath, pace and silence, to how you close. Most people lose a room in the first seven seconds or in the two seconds after their best line. You find out which one is yours.

Singing is part of it. Everyone in the room fails at the same time, which is the reason it is done in a group.

What to expect

Capability first.

These sessions are aimed at people who negotiate and hold hard conversations for a living. Founders, senior leaders, coaches and facilitators, educators, and anyone who reads people for a living. They work whether the method is new to you or familiar.

Where a session collects data, the anonymised results go back to everyone who attended. Where the research behind it is still small and self-reported, that gets said plainly in the room rather than dressed up.
