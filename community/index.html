Three ways to be in the room.

Empathy is not agreement. It is holding a perspective that is not yours long enough to actually understand it, and there are few faster ways to grow that than sitting with people who see the world nothing like you do. Everything here is a version of the same bet.

Why they sit together

Disagreement is not the problem. Pretending is.

A roundtable, a walkthrough of a waste room, and a drill on reading a hard conversation look like three different things. They are the same practice in three settings. Each one puts you somewhere you cannot stay abstract, next to people whose experience does not match yours, and asks you to stay in the room anyway.

That is Sphere 4 and Sphere 5 work, the affected indirect stakeholders and those who come after, made concrete. You cannot do it from behind a desk, and you cannot do it from a report. You have to go and be there.
