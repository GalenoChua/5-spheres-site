Skill Drills

One skill, ninety minutes, then ninety days.

Empathy is a skill. And like other skills, it is not acquired by being explained to you. Each drill takes one empathetic skill for you to practise — in a small group, with a hot seat, scoring yourself before and after. Drop in when you can, and log your theory and your hours practised towards certification.

For the founder, leader or professional who can't commit to an entire six or eight week course, but wants to sustain periodic practice.

How a drill works

The session is the easy part.

Most training ends at the end of the session, which is why most training changes nothing. A drill is built the other way round: the session exists to find the one thing you should work on, and then you go and work on it. Which means the end of the session is where your training begins.

01

The ninety minutes

A short piece of what the research says, stated with its limits. Then you practise in a small group and take the hot seat. You score yourself on the skill before and after, and we record it at the start and end of every session, so there is data to work with rather than a feeling about how it went.

€50 a session
02

The ninety days

You leave with your lowest score, a simple daily practice, and the anonymised before-and-after from the whole room. Ninety days is roughly how long it takes for a practice to stop being a thing you have to remember to do. If it helps, we can set you up with an accountability buddy from your group.

Your time
03 — needs 02

Supervision, if you want it

Without the practice there is nothing to supervise, so this only works on top of the ninety days. Send me recordings of yourself using the skill with real people, in real situations. If you can't record at work or at home, we design a real-life activity instead, and go through that one to one. This is how you find out whether you can do it, rather than whether you understood it.
